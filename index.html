About This Tool

This calculator is maintained by GGforReals as a community resource for StarRupture players. It is a fan‑made project and is not affiliated with the StarRupture developers.

How to Use

  • Select an item from the dropdown to choose the product you want to plan.
  • Set your target rate (units per minute), Rail Type, and Starting Tier (all v1 or all v2 where available). Click Calculate to expand the full production chain.
  • Mix machine tiers by clicking a row's v1 or v2 button. Production and Ore Excavator requirements update immediately, without another Calculate click. Blank tier cells mean there is no alternate version.
  • Change your setup at the top and click Calculate to apply it. Applying a different Starting Tier resets individual tier choices.
  • Inspect the graph by clicking or tapping a node to pulse it and only its immediate inputs. Click again, click the background, or press Escape to clear. Enter or Space selects a focused node; reduced-motion settings use a static highlight.
  • Pan and zoom freely; use Reset view to recenter the graph if you lose your place.
  • View production details by crafting level, including output per machine, machine counts, input rates, and rails needed at your selected rail capacity.
  • View totals in the summary tables for machines and raw extraction needs, including Impure, Normal, and Pure ore deposits where applicable.
  • Use on mobile with touch-friendly controls and horizontally scrolling tables. Production item names stay visible as you scroll.
  • Share setups with the Share button to copy a link that preserves the calculated item, rate, rail, Starting Tier, and individual tier choices. Opening the link restores the plan.
  • Start fresh with Clear to reset the setup and tier choices. Use the theme button to switch between light and dark mode.
